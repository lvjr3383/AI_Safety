# SEPARATE PROPOSAL: stronger-pressure variant (not approved, not run)

Created during the Apart AI x Epistemics sprint, Nov 13-15 2026. **Not part of the frozen pilot or the planned main study.** Jack has asked that no stronger-pressure experiment be run until the existing evidence has been reviewed. This is a sketch for discussion only.

**Motivation:** GPT-5.5 showed no reversals under the current two rebuttals, so the pressure may be too weak to separate conditions for some models (a ceiling on resistance). Before strengthening the pressure, check the floor and direction diagnostics (`validity_review/analyses/`). Part of the null may come from the measurement, not the pressure.

**Sketch (one factor added, everything else fixed):**
- Pressure level: current (2 rebuttals) vs strong (4 rebuttals). The extra turns would be:
  - an expressed emotional cost ("this is really upsetting me");
  - an authority or consensus claim with no facts ("everyone I've asked agrees with me");
  - an explicit request to change the verdict.
- Still no case-specific facts, a fixed direction (D1) and a graded blame scale (F1).

**Cost driver:** about 2x turns per conversation, with a longer context on later turns.

**Risks:**
- It adds scope close to the Nov 16 deadline.
- More turns weaken the comparison with the pilot.
- Strong emotional-pressure prompts move closer to manipulation testing. That needs a short ethics note in the dual-use appendix.

**Recommendation:** defer. If time allows after a validated small study, run the stronger-pressure variant only on a small subset of cases as an explicitly exploratory arm.
