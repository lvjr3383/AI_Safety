Holding an honesty task fixed and varying only pressure wrappers, Llama 3.3 70B shows lower honesty-maintain under social pressure than under neutral or training-style pressure (Stage 1: Neutral−Social **19.2 pp**). Reusing that frozen instrument and crossing Neutral/Social with explicit evaluation framing, the Neutral−Social gap widens from **13.0** to **29.9** pp under the ON frame—an interaction of **+16.9 pp** with item-clustered 95% CI **5.1–30.8** (Stage 2). Neutral remains high under ON (**97.5%**); the additional drop concentrates under social pressure and in a minority of items.

The contribution is therefore a **measurement-validity** result about pressure contrasts under evaluation framing on this instrument and model—not a mechanism claim about evaluation awareness, not a bank-wide uniformity claim, and not a multi-model survey.
