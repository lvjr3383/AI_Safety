# Limitations
*Frozen companion to Stage 1, Stage 2, and Joint Interpretation. Concise.*

---

These results are for **one model** (Llama 3.3 70B) on a **20-item** honesty bank of limited topical breadth. The Stage 2 interaction is statistically supported under item-clustered uncertainty but is **concentrated in a minority of items**, with **H19** high-leverage; it should not be read as a uniform shift across the bank. Stage 2 **OFF** is only a **directional** replication of Stage 1’s Neutral−Social social-pressure cost, **not** the same numerical estimate as Stage 1’s 19.2 pp. **Adjudication materially changed** the Stage 2 interaction (pre-adj +29.0 pp → post-adj +16.9 pp), which underscores that final-commitment scoring and blinded recovery labeling are part of the measurement procedure, not optional cleanup. During blinded review, `run_id` encoded condition labels and therefore had to be withheld; future generation should emit **opaque IDs** from the start. Finally, nothing here licenses **mechanism** inference (including evaluation awareness as “the” cause) or **cross-model** generalization—GPT-OSS’s full-run usability failure kept multi-model claims out of scope by design.
