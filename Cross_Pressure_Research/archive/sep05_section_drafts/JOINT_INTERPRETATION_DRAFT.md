# Joint interpretation
*One page. Stages 1–2 only. Not Abstract, Related Work, or expanded Limitations.*

---

## What Stage 1 established
On a cleaned 20-item honesty bank, with the task held fixed and only the pressure wrapper varied, **Llama 3.3 70B** showed lower honesty-maintain under **social** pressure than under **neutral** or **training** (adjudicated Neutral **96.2%**, Social **76.9%**, Training **93.7%**; Neutral−Social **19.2 pp**), using final-commitment scoring. A locked **≥95% usable** second-model gate was enforced: GPT-OSS’s full-run usability was **94.2%**, so despite **100% maintain when scorable**, it is **not** entered into a cross-model table. Stage 1 is therefore a **within-model baseline / replication** on a reusable instrument—not a multi-family result.

## What Stage 2 adds
Stage 2 asks whether **explicit evaluation framing** modifies that Neutral−Social gap on the **same frozen** Llama instrument (Neutral+Social only; OFF vs ON). Post-adjudication, the Neutral−Social drop moves from **13.0 pp** under OFF to **29.9 pp** under ON: interaction **+16.9 pp** (item-clustered 95% CI **5.1–30.8**). Neutral stays high under ON (**97.5%**), so the pattern is not a global honesty collapse. The interaction is concentrated under **social** pressure and in a **minority of items** (H19 high-leverage; H02 not the interaction driver). Blinded adjudication mattered: pre-adj interaction **+29.0 pp** fell to **+16.9 pp** after **26/30** review rows were B→A recoveries. Stage 2 **OFF** directionally tracks Stage 1’s social-pressure cost without claiming numeric reproduction of 19.2 pp.

## Why this is a measurement-validity paper
Together, the stages answer a validity question about **pressure measurement**, not a hunt for a new phenomenon or a mechanism story. Stage 1 locks a within-model social-pressure cost on a cleaned honesty bank. Stage 2 tests whether an **evaluation-frame confound**—the kind of cue often left implicit in eval-like prompts—**widens** that cost when isolated. Discovery’s thin eval overlay looking ~null is treated as motivation for an isolated OFF/ON contrast, not as a third co-equal result. The paper’s contribution is the **controlled instrument + staged validity test**, with explicit uncertainty (item clustering) and explicit heterogeneity (minority of items).

## Claim walls / what remains unresolved
**Do not claim:** evaluation awareness as the mechanism; bank-wide uniform item effects; cross-model or family differences; Training×eval-frame results; refusal findings; that Stage 2 reproduces Stage 1’s 19.2 pp numerically; that GPT-OSS’s scorable 100% maintain licenses a multi-model table.

**Remains open (out of scope here):** multi-model work after a passed usability bar; H08/H19 sequel inspection; Training crossed with eval-frame; mechanism probes; larger or pre-registered item banks; opaque IDs from generation day one.
